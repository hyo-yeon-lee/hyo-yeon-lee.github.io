I am a First-Class Computer Science graduate from the University of Bristol with a strong interest in linguistics (first-language acquisition), computational cognitive neuroscience, and computational modelling. I am currently working as a Research Intern at the Seoul National University, and will be joining the Max Planck School of Cognition Doctoral Programme as a fast-track candidate this September. Previously, I worked as a Research Intern at the Sony Computer Science Laboratories Kyoto on open-ended evolution, and a Research Support Assistant at the University of Bristol on a study investigating language transmission through ILM models.

My interest focuses on discovering what kind of biases in human brain makes us favour in building grammatical structure in language, and what advantages it brings us when it comes to generalisation abilities.
